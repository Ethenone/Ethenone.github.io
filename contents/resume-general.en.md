<h2 id="research">Selected Research Experience</h2>

### Fragment-level cfDNA methylation modeling for cancer detection

<p class="meta">Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Jun. 2026–present · Supervisor: Dr. Yani Kang</p>

- Developed a framework that combines deep learning and statistical evidence aggregation to detect tumors and estimate tumor fraction from partially observed methylation fragments.
- Designed simulation studies and compared the method with four published approaches; also evaluated it on an external thyroid cancer cohort, with attention to dependence among neighboring CpG sites.
- A first-author manuscript is under review.

### Conserved methylation states in single-cell clones

<p class="meta">Shanghai Jiao Tong University · May 2026–present · Supervisors: Dr. Yani Kang and Prof. Zhifeng Shao</p>

- Developed a Markov and likelihood-based model for RRBS data to distinguish persistent methylated or unmethylated CpG sites from stochastic observations.
- Built model-fitting and statistical analysis workflows for single-cell clone sequencing data.
- A co-first-author manuscript is in preparation.

### cfDNA methylation markers for head and neck cancer

<p class="meta">Shanghai Jiao Tong University · 2024–2025 · Supervisor: Dr. Yani Kang</p>

- Analyzed WGBS profiles from 205 samples across 39 healthy tissue types to identify head and neck tissue-specific methylation sites. Consolidated 1,157 candidate CpG sites into 11 regional markers.
- Integrated genomic annotation, specificity analysis, and visualization with targeted bisulfite sequencing of plasma cfDNA for experimental validation.
- The work led to a first-author paper and a published Chinese patent application.

### Cell segmentation and recognition in pathology images

<p class="meta">Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Sep. 2023–Mar. 2024 · Supervisor: Prof. Lichi Zhang</p>

- Reproduced and evaluated cell segmentation and abnormal-cell recognition methods for immunohistochemistry images.
- Combined recognition models with a multilayer perceptron and connected segmentation and recognition into one image-analysis pipeline.

### Lung cell-specific methylation marker analysis

<p class="meta">Shanghai Jiao Tong University · Mar.–Jun. 2024 · Supervisor: Dr. Yani Kang</p>

- Used wgbstools to screen cell-specific methylation sites in lung tissue; performed gene annotation, functional analysis, clustering, and visualization.
